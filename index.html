Petrol Facts

What is petrol?

Petrol is a liquid fuel used in spark-ignition engines. It is produced mainly from crude oil.

What is mileage?

Mileage tells you how far a vehicle travels per litre of fuel. Higher mileage generally means less fuel is needed for the same distance.

What is octane rating?

Octane rating measures petrol's resistance to engine knocking. Follow your vehicle manufacturer's recommended fuel grade.
